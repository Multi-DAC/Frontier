## 0. WHY THIS SECTION EXISTS, AND WHY IT GOES NEAR THE FRONT

The premise is: **there are entities in our world that humans evolved not to perceive, because
perceiving them was disadvantageous** (Clayton Iggulden-Schnell, D201 18:52; mechanism restored as
`PREREGISTRATION.md §1b` Mechanism 4, *adaptive non-perception*).

That premise **explains its own absence of evidence.** Every null is available for absorption: *of
course you found nothing — the not-finding is the phenomenon.* A hypothesis that consumes its own
disconfirmation is not a weak hypothesis, it is **not a hypothesis**, and no amount of pre-registration
elsewhere in the paper repairs it. This is the failure mode of the genre we chose and this premise is
maximally exposed to it.

So the section goes **near the front, in the reader's hands, before the evidence** — not in a
limitations paragraph at the end, where a falsifier is a courtesy rather than a commitment.

**The argument of this section in one line:**

> ⭐ **The obscuration is not free. It is a mechanism, mechanisms are built, built things make positive
> predictions, and positive predictions can fail.** Everything below is a consequence of that sentence.

## 1. THE DECOMPOSITION — the premise is two claims, and only one of them is self-sealing

| | Claim | Self-sealing? |
|---|---|---|
| **(A)** | The **entities** exist | ✅ **Yes.** Any null is absorbable by (B). Attacking this directly is a trap |
| **(B)** | Human non-perception of them is **evolved and selected-for** | ❌ **No.** This is a claim about *us* |

⚠ **SCOPE CORRECTION — D202 ~21:2x, `PREREGISTRATION.md §7e`, on the premise author's own instruction.**
Claim **(B) does not cover the whole class.** The class splits at a level above this table:
**GROUP I — APERTURAL** (the signal never reaches a human transducer: too small, too big, wrong
timescale, out of band, weakly coupled) and **GROUP II — ADAPTIVE** (it reaches one and is centrally
gated — Mechanism 4). **(B) is a claim about Group II alone.** Group I's non-perception has no
evolutionary explanation and needs none; being small is free.

**Every falsifier below except F4 and F6 targets Group II, and this is the section's real load.**
Group II is where the paper's novelty lives — Group I is Davies-shaped, a claim about instruments —
and Group II is also the branch **with no measurements on it.** ⛔ A Group II null therefore may
**not** be answered with *"then it was Group I,"* and a Group I null may not be answered with
*"then it was Group II."* See **forbidden move #9**; it is the same crossing as #3 in a new costume,
and it was pinned before the Group II leg opened.

**(B) is the crack, and it is the premise author's own emphasis.** He did not propose "there are
things we can't see" — that is trivially true and predicts nothing. He proposed that **the not-seeing
is adaptive**, which is a claim about human sensory and neural architecture, testable on human
subjects and human tissue, **with no access to the entities required.**

⭐ **This is why the repair in `PREREGISTRATION.md §1b` mattered so much.** *Not selected for* predicts
a **hole** — and holes are unfalsifiable, because absence of a sensor explains any null forever.
*Selected against* predicts **machinery** — funded, present, inspectable, and **absent-able.** The
premise author's version is the more falsifiable one. Every falsifier below except F4 and F6 exists
because of that difference.

## 2. THE FALSIFIERS

### F1 — THE MACHINERY FALSIFIER · *targets (B) directly*

Active suppression is **expensive**. You do not fund a transducer for a signal you then always
discard, unless the discarding is itself the adaptive function. Not-having-a-sensor is free;
having-a-sensor-and-gating-it is paid for every generation.

**Positive prediction.** For a candidate channel *C*, there exists in human physiology:
1. **transduction** of *C* — a receiver, and
2. **central gating** of that transduced signal, and
3. gating that is **specific to *C***, not explained by the general bandwidth economy that discards
   most of everything.

**⛔ FALSIFIED IF:** across the candidate channels, humans show **plain absence of transduction.** No
receiver means no suppression, means no adaptive non-perception, means Mechanism 4 is dead and the
premise collapses to the trivial reading in §1.

**⚠ The honest weakness, stated by us and not left for a referee.** Condition (3) is the whole test
and it is the hard one. Humans gate enormous quantities of transduced signal; demonstrating *that*
gating occurs is nearly free and proves nothing. **Without a specificity criterion pinned before
looking, F1 is unfalsifiable in practice** — it would confirm on any nervous system. Any deployment
of F1 must pre-register what would distinguish *C*-specific gating from generic economy, or F1 does
not run.

**Grade: testable now, against existing sensory neuroscience. No new instrument. No entity required.**

---

### F2 — THE PHYLOGENETIC FALSIFIER · *targets (B); makes the premise pay a historical debt*

Selection is a **historical event** and historical events leave comparative signatures. If blindness
to *C* was selected for **in our lineage** because perceiving *C* was costly **to us**, the blindness
should be **derived, not ancestral.**

**Positive prediction.** Animals in overlapping niches without that specific pressure — and sister
taxa generally — should **retain** sensitivity to *C*.

**⛔ FALSIFIED IF:** insensitivity to *C* is **ancestral and clade-universal.** If nothing perceives
*C*, there was no selective event. The channel simply sits outside animal sensory range, which is
**physics, not adaptation**, and the premise's distinctive content — that this is about *us* — is
gone.

**⚠ The symmetric trap, closed now.** Finding an animal that *does* perceive *C* confirms **the
channel's perceptibility only.** It is not evidence for the biome. These are separate propositions
and no sentence in the paper may let the first carry the second.

---

### F3 — THE TAIL FALSIFIER · ⭐ *the one that nails the self-sealing shut*

Negative selection on a perceptual capacity, unless ancient and absolute, **leaves variance.**
Mutation–selection balance, standing variation, and relaxed selection in modern environments all
predict a low-frequency subpopulation that **still perceives.**

⭐ **And this binds Mechanism 4 specifically — the premise author's own version.** A *loss* that has
gone to fixation is hard to reverse; restoring it needs new mutation and that is slow, so a pure-loss
mechanism could legitimately show no tail. But Mechanism 4 is not loss — it is an **active gate**, and
gates are **regulated**, and regulated things **vary between individuals.** The version of the premise
that predicts machinery is the version that must also predict a tail. It does not get to have the
first without the second.

**Positive prediction.** A subpopulation performs **above chance** on a forced-choice task in which an
**instrument independently registers *C***. Detector-anchored. **Never self-report.**

**⛔ FALSIFIED IF:** no above-chance subpopulation exists under that protocol at adequate power.

> ### ⛔⛔ THE RESCUE THAT IS NOT AVAILABLE
>
> A null in F3 **may not be absorbed by the obscuration.** The obscuration mechanism **predicts the
> tail.** To explain away an F3 null, one must claim the blindness went to **fixation** — and that is
> a **strictly stronger claim than the premise licenses.** It requires its own argument and its own
> evidence, on the record, as a *revision* of the premise.
>
> **It is not free, it is not implied, and it may not be reached for after the data comes back.**
> This paragraph is the single load-bearing sentence of this document: it is the specific place where
> the self-sealing move is closed by name, in advance, rather than by good intentions.

**Already operationalized.** This is exactly what locus **L2** measures — camera-trap blanks,
`PREREG-TERRESTRIAL.md §2`: frames where **humans said blank** and a detector with no evolutionary
history said **something**, independently produced. It is the only locus in the entire program where
the **human is inside the instrument**, which is why it, and not L1, is the actual test.

### F4 — THE DISSIPATION FALSIFIER · *targets (A), via physics*

⭐ **Life is dissipative. Anything alive must couple to something.** "Non-interacting" is not on the
menu — it is excluded by what it means to be alive, not by our instruments. ⚠ **The foundation of this
falsifier rests on an OWED citation:** `§7c` attributes it to *Schrödinger 1944* (negative entropy) and
*Prigogine* (dissipative structures) and marks both **⚠ OWED** — neither has been read at source. F4 is
the falsifier with the strongest-sounding premise and the weakest citation state in this document, and
that combination is worth naming out loud. `PREREGISTRATION.md §7c`
splits the premise author's *"lacking interaction with matter"* into **B1** (weak coupling, reachable)
and **B2** (own sector) — and **B2 still gravitates**, because stress–energy is not optional. Branch B
is therefore a **hard measurement**, not an unfalsifiable category.

**⛔ FALSIFIED IF:** for a proposed locus, the conserved-quantity budget **closes with no residual at
the achieved sensitivity.** Then either nothing is there, or it sits below a floor — **and the floor
is a published number, not a shrug.**

**⚠ Current state, and it is sensitivity, not detection.** A 350 g mass at 1 m computes to **2.34×** a
superconducting gravimeter's static floor. **Tidal, atmospheric and hydrological loading are
uncomputed.** ⛔ **No gravimetric detection claim exists and none may be written** until the loading
terms are run. `PAPER-00-ARCHITECTURE.md §14`.

**⚠ Scope limit, stated by us.** F4 can only ever **exclude regions of parameter space.** It cannot
falsify the premise entire, and the paper must not imply otherwise. What it buys is that the
excluded region is *numbered* — which converts "you can never test this" into "here is what has been
tested, and here is what it cost."

### F5 — THE FOLKLORE INVERSION · *the evidence that runs backwards*

**Selected-for blindness predicts an ABSENCE in the cultural record.** If humans were selected not to
perceive *C*, the record should be **quiet** about *C*.

⭐ **Therefore a rich, identity-dense folklore corpus about invisible entities is evidence AGAINST
this premise, not for it.** The material a reader will expect us to cite as support is material that
**disqualifies its own subject.** Stating this before opening the corpus is the strongest available
signal that the paper is not reasoning backwards from a conclusion.

Pre-committed in `PREREGISTRATION.md §7d` as two outcomes — **and they belong to different mechanisms,
which I flattened on the first draft of this line and caught while verifying it:**

- **FA — silent record.** §7d's *"no transducer"* case. ⚠ **This is the §1a-style mechanism — the one
  that was WITHDRAWN.** A silent record is evidence for the *narrowed* premise, not the live one.
- **FB — altered-state-concentrated, identity-poor.** §7d's *active gating* case — **Mechanism 4, the
  live one, the premise author's.** Its logic is sharp: the edit is metabolically funded, so it should
  **fail when the funding drops** — sleep, fever, hypoxia, sensory deprivation, psychedelics, dying.
  Recurrent structural motifs with **no stable taxonomy**, because a stable named identity requires
  repeated *waking* consensus to accumulate.
- **A rich, identity-dense, waking-reported record — inconsistent with both. ⛔ FALSIFIED.**

⭐ **Consequence, and it is not the obvious one: under the live mechanism, FA is not a win either.**
Mechanism 4 predicts **FB specifically.** A silent record would support the mechanism we withdrew.
So F5 has *two* ways to fire against the live premise — a record that is too rich, **and** a record
that is too quiet — and only the narrow middle shape confirms. That is a much tighter target than
"the record is consistent with obscuration," which is what a flattened version of this line would
have let us claim.

*(Renamed FA/FB here. `§7d` labels these **F1/F2**, which collides with F1 and F2 of this document —
caught while writing this line, and a collision between two live pre-registered label sets is exactly
the kind of thing that becomes a real misreading three drafts later. `§7d`'s own text is unchanged;
this is a display alias and is flagged as one.)*

⛔ **FORBIDDEN MOVE #3 binds:** may not score presence **and** absence in the same corpus.

**⚠ And the premise author's constraint is honoured throughout:** *"not a census… the premise, not any
specific identities."* **No entity is ever a candidate.** The corpus is evidence about *the record*,
never about *what is in the world*.

### F7 — THE TAPE FALSIFIER · ⭐ *targets Group II; added D202 ~21:2x, and it closes an escape rather than opening one*

**Derived, not imported.** Group II says the signal **is transduced** by a human receiver and then
discarded **centrally**. A signal in a band a human retina or cochlea transduces is, by construction,
**in band for any instrument that shares that band.** The gate is in the head; the photons are not.

**Positive prediction.** For a centrally-gated channel *C*, an instrument transducing *C* — an
ordinary camera, an ordinary microphone — **records the entity.** Group II entities are **on the
tape.**

**⛔ FALSIFIED IF:** high-coverage, high-quality recording in *C*'s band, over the relevant volume,
is **empty.**

⭐ **Why this is a cost and not a hedge.** It means **Group II ⊆ Branch A** (`PREREGISTRATION.md §2`,
§7e): the adaptive branch **may never retreat to "not device-renderable."** That retreat exists for
Group I Axis 2 and for nothing else. The branch carrying the paper's whole novelty is also the branch
with the least room to run.

**⚠ The honest weakness, same standing as F1's.** *"Empty"* is doing heavy work and the entity's
amplitude, rate and volume are unpinned — so **F7 has no specificity criterion yet and cannot fire as
written.** Any deployment must pre-register a detection floor and a coverage denominator first.
⛔ **And the six ZTF passes may not be scored against it retroactively:** they are optical, therefore
in principle Group II-relevant, but no Group II prediction was registered before they ran. They are a
**prior deployment of F7's logic**, cited as precedent — never as a result.

**Grade: derivable now; not deployable until its floor is pinned.**

## 4. WHAT SURVIVES IF EVERY FALSIFIER FIRES — the paper's own death condition

Stated plainly, now, while nothing is at stake:

⚠ **AMENDED D202 ~21:2x — the first bullet was FALSE, and false in the direction that flatters us.**
It said a Group II null killed *"the premise's entire distinctive content,"* which reads as the whole
paper dying honestly. It does not: Group I survives untouched, because Group I never claimed
machinery. **The bullet was too generous to our rigour and too harsh on the outcome, and the gap
between those two is exactly where the escape hatch sits.** Restated with the branch line drawn:

- **If F1 and F2 both fire** — no *C*-specific gating machinery, and the insensitivity is
  clade-universal — then **claim (B) is dead**, and with it **GROUP II**, which is **the whole of
  this paper's novelty**; what survives is Group I, which is Davies 2009's territory and not ours.
  ⛔ **The forbidden sentence, written out so it cannot be arrived at innocently:** *"the premise
  survives — it was Group I all along."* Group I surviving a test it was never subject to is **not**
  the premise surviving. That is forbidden move **#9**.
- **If F7 fires** — human-band recording, adequately floored and covered, is empty — Group II is dead
  from the other side, and it may **not** retreat to Branch B. It never had that door (§7e).
- **If Group I's own tests return null** — and at the one deep locus they have — that null **stays in
  Group I.** It is not evidence for Group II and never becomes evidence for Group II by elimination.
  ⛔ Elimination between two branches neither of which has passed anything is not an argument.
- **If F3 fires** and F1/F2 hold, the premise survives **only** in a fixation form that must be argued
  separately and has not been.
- **If F4 fires** at a locus, that locus is **priced and closed**. Others are not.
- **If F5 fires** — a rich, identity-dense record — the *adaptive* reading is in serious trouble
  regardless of everything else, because the record should have been quiet.
- **F6 has already partly fired**, and the honest consequence was a withdrawn survey row and a demoted
  existence proof. **That is what this section looks like when it works.**

⭐ **The paper's claim is not that a shadow biome exists.** It is that **the premise is a real
question, it is reachable with instruments that already exist, and here is the map of how to lose.**
A hypothesis paper earns its genre by handing the reader the falsifiers — and by showing the places
where its own falsifiers already cost the authors something.
