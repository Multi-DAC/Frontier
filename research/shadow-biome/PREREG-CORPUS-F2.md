## 0. THE RULING, UP FRONT

**Admitted — but on the arm that was already written down, and at the price that arm charges.**

His justification is not a new criterion. *"Rarely perceived / perception considered anomalous"* is a
claim about **state-of-report**, not about entity identity — which is exactly and only what §7d
pre-committed as scorable, before he said it and before anything was opened. He selected **F2**
without seeing F2.

That matters in one direction that helps and one that costs:

- **Helps:** the request cannot be a relaxation of a gauge, because the gauge already had this arm
  and it can still lose. Nothing was widened to admit him.
- **Costs:** `FORBIDDEN MOVE #3` binds. **F1 and F2 make opposite predictions about the same corpus
  and exactly one may be scored.** Taking F2 *spends* the inversion — the finding that a large
  folklore corpus disqualifies its subject. We may keep it as an argument about *other people's*
  use of the record; we may not keep it as evidence for ours while also citing presence.

**The bare fact that the Corpus exists and is large counts for nothing here. Volume is F1's
disconfirmation, not F2's confirmation. Only the *distribution* can score.**

## 0b. THE GUARD ON "EVERY RULE HAS ITS EXCEPTIONS"

Unguarded, that sentence is the end of the paper. A hypothesis whose absence-of-evidence is explained
by the mechanism *and* whose presence-of-evidence is explained by exceptions to the mechanism has no
possible observation against it. That is the failure mode `PAPER-01-GENRE.md §4` names as fatal.

The guard is not to refuse exceptions. It is that **the exception channel must have a shape committed
before it is looked at**, and the shape must be one the record could fail to have. §7d already
specified it: *altered-state-concentrated, identity-poor.* This file operationalizes that and adds
what the fiction and the mechanism jointly predict. Any exception discovered *after* looking, and
admitted because it is needed, is a violation of this file.

## 3. PRE-COMMITTED PREDICTIONS — F2 ARM

Each states what would make it **fail**. A prediction with no failure condition is not registered.

**C1 — IDENTITY-POVERTY.** The record should be **motif-rich and taxonomy-poor**: recurrent
structural motifs recurring across independent source clusters, with no stable named identity carried
between them. A stable named identity requires repeated *waking* consensus to accumulate, which F2
forbids.
→ **FAILS IF** a small set of stable, elaborated, cross-source-consistent named taxa carries the bulk
of report mass. That is the folklore signature and it is F1's disqualification winning.

**C2 — STATE-OF-REPORT SKEW. ⛔ REGISTERED AND NOT RUNNABLE ON THIS CORPUS.**
F2 predicts reports concentrate where the metabolic funding of the edit drops. **This prediction
cannot be scored on the Corpus-Perspectival**, because that corpus was *assembled on the property
being tested* — Clayton's own justification says so: the things in it are there *because* their
perception is considered anomalous. Scoring concentration on a corpus selected for concentration is
circular. *(feedback_self_generated_denominator.)*
→ Scoring C2 requires an **unselected comparison corpus** (Thompson's Motif-Index, HRAF, or an
ordinary-perception baseline). Named as **OWED**, not as a null.

**C3 — AVERSIVENESS.** If perceiving was selected *against*, the exception's phenomenology should
skew dread/threat rather than wonder/comfort. The cost has to show up somewhere.
→ **FAILS IF** affect across the record is predominantly benign, awe-positive or instructional.

**C4 — SOCIAL ENFORCEMENT (the Dozois prediction).** If the blindness is maintained at the social
layer rather than only the sensory one, reporters should carry a **measurable cost**: pathologization,
medicalization, retraction, credibility loss, career damage — not mere disbelief.
→ **FAILS IF** reporting is socially cheap, or if the recorded costs are indistinguishable from the
ordinary cost of any low-prior claim.

⚠ C4 is a prediction about *humans and institutions*. If it holds it is evidence for a **social**
suppression mechanism and **is not, by itself, evidence for a biome.** Any draft sentence that lets a
held C4 imply otherwise violates `PREREGISTRATION.md §2`'s FORBIDDEN MOVE, exactly as
`PREREG-TERRESTRIAL.md §1.4` binds T3/T4.
