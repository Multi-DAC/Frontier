## 1. Beni, M.D. (2021) — "A critical analysis of Markovian monism." *Synthese.*
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11229-021-03075-x · PMID 33612868 · (assessment ref 31, "A critical analysis of Markovian monism").
- **VERIFIED claim (from abstract):** FEP was extended into *Markovian monism* (Friston et al. 2020, *Behavioral & Brain Sciences* 102:227–260). Beni is skeptical of the **arguments** for MM. Core critique: Markov blankets are **scientific models**; you cannot read metaphysical theses about the *target* systems off theories about the nature of the *models*. He considers MM's blankets as material / fictional / mathematical entities and argues **none** of these readings supports a metaphysical stance — *neither neutral monism nor reductive physicalism*. "Properties of Markovian models — such as being neither physical nor mental — could not be projected onto their targets to determine the ontological properties of targets."
- **★ ASSESSMENT DISCREPANCY (LC59 catch #2 on the assessment):** the assessment called this a *"circularity fallacy"* (MM "assumes a dual information geometry to explain epistemic access, delivering only what it presupposed"). That is **not** Beni's actual argument. Beni's argument is a **model→target projection fallacy** (map-vs-territory), not circularity. Do not cite it as "circularity."
- **★ DOUBLE-EDGED — the important part.** Beni's blade cuts against MM's metaphysical over-reach, but it *also* cuts against **our** move if we frame it as "we avoid MM's problem by grounding the blanket in dual-aspect neutral monism" — that would be reading metaphysics off the model too. **Framing correction for the chapter:** the Markov blanket is *only* the mathematical description of how a localized stream manages its boundary (dynamics), NOT a source of the neutral monism. The neutral monism is **Axiom 1**, posited independently. This is Beni-safe AND cleaner: "active inference is not the creator of mind and matter — it is the boundary-dynamics of a stream whose two-aspect nature is given by A1." (This matches the assessment's *better* sentence, and repudiates its *weaker* one.)

## 2. Chis-Ciure, R. (2025) — "Consciousness science and constitutive a priori principles: on the fundamental identity of integrated information theory." *Philosophical Psychology* (Taylor & Francis).
