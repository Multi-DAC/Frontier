## The convention that broke

`Research/basement-drafts/`, `Research/fresh-eyes/`, and `Research/sources/` were all
Corpus-Perspectival conventions. The Day-166 archive retired the repo but **not the habit** —
so the writing continued into a tree that could no longer receive it. This is the same shape
as the Drift essay publish break found the same morning: *the destination changed, the reflex
didn't.*

**Placement here is a rescue, not a ruling.** Whether Frontier's `research/` is the right
permanent home for the sources register and the fresh-eyes reviews is Clayton's call. What was
not negotiable was leaving them unbacked while that decision waited — deferring care until a
decision arrives is exactly [[LC64]], and the decision can arrive at any time now that the
files are safe.
