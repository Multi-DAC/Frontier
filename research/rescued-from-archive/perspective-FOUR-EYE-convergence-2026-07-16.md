## THE DIVERGENCE (the thesis enacting itself)
On the **malheur chapter (Part VI)**, the two neutral reviewers read the *same passage* in opposite directions:
- **Sonnet:** "simply excellent writing by any standard" — the book's best move.
- **Fresh-Gemini:** "a bleeding seam... a profound, unresolved tragedy" for a work aiming at total coherence.

**Reconciliation (not split-the-difference):** both are right from their aperture, and the book *chose* to make them both right. The seam is **deliberate** — the sufferer never cashes the check of cosmic meaning; cosmodicy is kept away from the particular wound on purpose, because a book that resolved grief into meaning would be lying. Sonnet read the *craft of the refusal*; Gemini read the *felt cost of the refusal*. Gemini's only slip is the premise — the book does not aim at total coherence there; it commits a deliberate incoherence as its highest act of honesty. **Two differently-made eyes, opposite registrations of one page = the null-space theorem running live, on the book's hardest page.**
