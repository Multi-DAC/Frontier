# CANDIDATE LIST — place-threshold screen

*Built 2026-08-18. Rendered at the resolution A7 measured, not at the resolution the score is printed to.*

## What this list is

A search plan. Places ranked by a geophysical criterion whose predictive validity is UNDEMONSTRATED and, on the one external test run (A6), unsupported.

**A6, the one external test:** The screen's founding gate (R1 CONDUIT, <=10 km to a Quaternary normal fault) was asked once whether it fires where anomalous lights are independently reported. 0 of 6 control locales pass it; 34.4% of random western ground does. p=0.199 on the distance contrast, direction against the hypothesis. A fail at n=6 is not proof of absence, and it is not support either.

**A7, the resolution:** A7: one integer position resolved, a head-set of 4 with unresolved internal order, a band of 23 the score cannot order. One L1 vertex = 0.025 score.

> **JURISDICTION FILTER: NOT RUN.** The founding claim's Forest Service predicate has not been applied to these rows. The column below reads UNRUN and must not be read as 'no constraint'.

## AREAS — the coarser question, which the data answers better

The screen emits faults; A7 says the fault-level order is not resolved. Single-linkage clustering of the head and band at **the frozen list's own 50 km separation scale** — not a new free parameter — collapses 23 named structures into **13 areas**. Areas containing a head site are listed first; within that, alphabetically.

> **Member count is not evidence.** It inherits the same mapping-density artefact A7-iv priced in the junction term: a valley mapped at 1:24,000 reports more distinct fault names than the same structure mapped once at 1:250,000. ## Footnotes

1. **score is printed and does not order the table.** Four of its five terms take 4–7 distinct values across the 225 survivors and one of them (slip) is the same number for 86% of the field. The composite is a coarse lattice; differences below one lattice step are not differences.
2. P(in top ten) under ±1 vertex of `quartz_frac`, 10,000 draws — the noise the quartz layer declared about itself before this question was asked. `<0.05` is A7's reporting floor for sites outside the frozen ten, and means measured-and-small, not unmeasured.
3. Three-valued. UNMEASURED is not OUT. UNRUN is not 'unconstrained'.

**Not in this table and belonging in the report:** the 1959 Hebgen Lake rupture supplies the three highest raw scores in the whole 225 (Madison, Red Canyon, Hebgen — 11.6 and 13.7 km apart). The 50 km separation rule prints it once. The screen's strongest signal is one earthquake, counted three times.
